**Work remotely from Spain with legal residency for you and your family.** We handle the International Teleworker Visa (or residence authorisation) end to end: from checking you qualify to collecting your TIE card.

## Who it is for

- Employees of foreign companies who can work remotely (the company must have been active for ≥ 1 year).
- Self-employed professionals and freelancers with clients outside Spain (at most 20% of income from Spanish companies).
- Families: your spouse or partner and children can be included in the same application.

## Key requirements (Law 28/2022)

| Requirement | What is required |
|---|---|
| Income | ≥ 200% of the Spanish minimum wage (≈ €2,400/month in 2026); +75% for the first family member and +25% for each additional one |
| Qualification | University degree or ≥ 3 years of professional experience |
| Work relationship | ≥ 3 months with the company/clients; express authorisation to work remotely |
| Health insurance | Full coverage in Spain |
| Criminal record | Certificate covering the last 5 years |

Not sure whether you qualify? Use the [eligibility checker](/en/digital-nomad-visa-eligibility-checker/) (2 minutes, no personal data).

## What our service includes

1. **Eligibility analysis** and choice of route: visa at the consulate (1 year) or residence authorisation from Spain (3 years).
2. **File preparation**: contract, company certificates, proof of income, insurance, criminal record, apostilles and sworn translations.
3. **Submission and follow-up** with the Large Companies Unit (UGE) — decision within 20 working days — or the consulate.
4. **Family**: joint applications for spouse and children.
5. **After approval**: fingerprint appointment, TIE card, town-hall registration and, if relevant, the [Beckham Law](/en/services/beckham-law-tax-advisory/) option (24% flat tax).

## How we work

First evaluation is **without obligation**: tell us your case through the form and within 24 working hours we confirm whether you qualify, which documents you need and a fixed quote. From then on, a single point of contact until your TIE.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the Digital Nomad Visa take?
From Spain (UGE) the decision comes within 20 working days of a complete submission; at a consulate usually 15–45 days depending on the country. Preparing the file normally takes 2–4 weeks.

### Can I apply while already in Spain as a tourist?
Yes. If you are legally in Spain (for example within the 90 Schengen days) you can apply directly for the 3-year residence authorisation with the UGE.

### Can I pay tax under the Beckham Law?
Yes, international teleworkers can opt for the impatriate regime (24% up to €600,000) if they have not been Spanish tax residents in the previous 5 years. We assess it in the same evaluation.
