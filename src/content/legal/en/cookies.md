<h1>Cookie Policy</h1>
<p><em>Last updated: 12 September 2026</em></p>
<p>This policy explains which cookies <strong>spaingh.com</strong> uses, for what purpose, and how you can accept, reject or change your choice at any time. It complements our <a href="/en/privacy-policy/">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
<h2>What are cookies?</h2>
<p>Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They are used to remember your preferences or to measure, in aggregate, how the site is used.</p>

<h2>Consent and how to change your mind</h2>
<p>A notice is shown when you enter the site. Analytics cookies are <strong>not activated</strong> until you click “Accept”. You can change your decision at any time:</p>
<p><a href="#" class="sgh-btn-cookies" data-cookie-settings>Change my cookie preferences</a></p>
<p>You can also delete cookies from your browser settings; the notice will then be shown again on your next visit.</p>
