**Hire or relocate to Spain in weeks, not months.** The Highly Qualified Professional residence authorisation (Law 14/2013) is the fast track for executives, technical staff and graduates hired by companies in Spain: decision within 20 working days, family included, no labour-market test.

## Who it is for

- **Companies** hiring foreign talent (large companies, strategic SMEs and startups).
- **Executives and graduates** with a job offer in Spain.
- **Families**: spouse and children are processed together and the spouse may work.

## 2026 requirements

| Profile | Condition |
|---|---|
| Managers and directors (group 1) | Gross annual salary ≥ €54,142 |
| Technical staff and graduates (group 2) | Gross annual salary ≥ €40,077 |
| Qualification | University degree or ≥ 3 years of equivalent experience |
| Employer | Contract of ≥ 1 year; company up to date with tax and Social Security |

Full guide to requirements, timelines and documents: [Highly Qualified Professional Visa Spain 2026](/en/highly-qualified-professional-visa-spain/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does the Highly Qualified Professional permit take?
The UGE-CE decides within 20 working days of a complete submission. Preparing the file usually takes 2–3 weeks.

### Can the company hire before the candidate is in Spain?
Yes. The employer applies for the authorisation from Spain and, once approved, the candidate obtains the visa at their consulate within days.

### Can my spouse work?
Yes. Family members reunited through this route receive authorisation to work in Spain.
